Quick fixes

Troubleshooting

Reminder notifications aren't arriving

  1. Open your device Settings → Notifications → 100Daysz and make sure notifications are allowed.
  2. Check that Do Not Disturb or a focus mode isn't silencing the app.
  3. On Android, disable battery optimization for 100Daysz so the OS doesn't stop scheduled reminders in the background.
  4. Re-open the 100Daysz app and re-enable your reminder time in Settings → Reminders so it re-schedules correctly.

My streak or challenge progress is missing

  1. Challenge data is stored offline-first, directly on your device — reinstalling the app or switching devices without a backup will not carry it over.
  2. If you're signed in to an account, confirm you're signed in with the same email you originally used.
  3. Avoid clearing the app's storage/cache from your device's app settings, as this removes locally saved progress.
  4. Still missing something? Email support@100daysz.com with your account email and roughly when the issue started.

Narrated audio isn't playing

  1. Check your device's ring/media volume and make sure it isn't muted or on silent.
  2. Disconnect and reconnect Bluetooth headphones, or try playing without them.
  3. Close other apps that may be holding the audio session (e.g. music or calls).
  4. Force-quit and reopen 100Daysz, then re-enter the breathing session or workout.

Subscription or purchase issue

  1. Subscriptions are billed and managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, not by 100Daysz directly.
  2. To cancel or change a plan, go to your platform's subscription settings (App Store or Google Play).
  3. For a missing purchase, make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID / Google account used to buy it, then restart the app.
  4. If the issue persists, email us with your purchase receipt and we'll help investigate.

Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account or data?

Use the in-app account deletion option, or email us — full steps are on the Account & Data Deletion page.

Can I use 100Daysz on more than one device?

You can install 100Daysz on multiple devices and sign in with the same account to manage your subscription. Local challenge data stays on the device where it was created.

How do I report a bug?

Email support@100daysz.com with your device model, OS version, and the steps that led to the problem — screenshots or screen recordings help a lot.

Is there a web version of 100Daysz?

Not currently. 100Daysz is a mobile app for iPhone and Android, built with Flutter.
