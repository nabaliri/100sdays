This Privacy Policy explains how 100Daysz LLC, a California limited liability company ("100Daysz," "Company," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects information in connection with the 100Daysz mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the information we collect and how we use it.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the Service and does not apply to third-party websites, applications, or services that have their own privacy policies.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use the Service and the features you choose to use.

We may collect the following categories of information.

2. Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name;
  • Email address;
  • Username;
  • Account identifier;
  • Authentication information;
  • Profile information; and
  • Other information necessary to establish and maintain your account.

3. Age and Date of Birth

100Daysz may collect your date of birth or age information.

We may use this information to:

  • Determine whether you meet the minimum age requirement;
  • Verify eligibility to use the Service;
  • Comply with applicable laws and platform requirements;
  • Protect the Service and its users;
  • Provide age-appropriate functionality where applicable; and
  • Maintain accurate account information.

Where possible, we may calculate your age from your date of birth rather than requiring you to separately provide both your date of birth and age.

We do not use your date of birth for purposes materially different from those described in this Privacy Policy without providing additional notice or obtaining consent where required by law.

4. Gender and Demographic Information

100Daysz may allow you to voluntarily provide demographic information, including gender.

Gender information may be optional.

If you choose to provide it, we may use it to:

  • Personalize certain features or experiences;
  • Improve the Service;
  • Understand general user demographics;
  • Analyze aggregate usage patterns; and
  • Provide other functionality disclosed at the time of collection.

You may use features that do not require gender information without providing it.

5. Challenge and Activity Information

When you use 100Daysz, we may collect information that you voluntarily enter into the Service, including:

  • Challenge names;
  • Challenge descriptions;
  • Challenge duration;
  • Challenge frequency;
  • Goals;
  • Completion records;
  • Streaks;
  • Progress information;
  • Notes; and
  • Other information associated with your challenges.

We use this information primarily to provide the challenge creation, tracking, progress, and personalization features of 100Daysz.

You are responsible for the information you choose to enter into your challenges and notes.

6. Device and Technical Information

Depending on the technologies used in the version of the Service you access, we may collect:

  • Device type;
  • Operating system;
  • Operating-system version;
  • App version;
  • IP address;
  • Device identifiers;
  • Language and regional settings;
  • Crash information;
  • Performance information;
  • Diagnostic information; and
  • Other technical information necessary to operate, secure, and improve the Service.

7. Usage Information

We may collect information about how you interact with the Service, such as:

  • Features used;
  • Screens viewed;
  • Buttons or functions used;
  • Session information;
  • App performance;
  • Error information; and
  • Other interaction information.

8. Purchase and Subscription Information

If you purchase a subscription or other paid feature, payment processing may be handled by Apple, Google, or another authorized payment provider (such as RevenueCat, which we use to manage subscription status across platforms).

100Daysz generally does not need to receive or store your full payment-card number when purchases are processed through Apple App Store or Google Play.

We may receive:

  • Subscription status;
  • Product purchased;
  • Purchase date;
  • Renewal status;
  • Transaction identifiers; and
  • Other information necessary to verify and manage your subscription or purchase.

9. Customer Support Information

If you contact us, we may collect:

  • Name;
  • Email address;
  • Account information;
  • Device or technical information you provide;
  • Contents of your support request; and
  • Other information you voluntarily provide.

10. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide the Service;
  • Create and maintain accounts;
  • Verify age eligibility;
  • Store challenges and progress;
  • Track challenge completion;
  • Personalize the user experience;
  • Provide relevant Ideas or suggested challenges;
  • Provide customer support;
  • Process and manage subscriptions;
  • Authenticate users;
  • Maintain security;
  • Detect fraud or abuse;
  • Diagnose technical problems;
  • Analyze Service performance;
  • Improve existing features;
  • Develop new features;
  • Understand general usage patterns;
  • Communicate with users regarding the Service;
  • Comply with legal obligations; and
  • Protect the rights, safety, and property of 100Daysz, users, or others.

11. Personalization

If the Service uses age, age range, gender, challenge history, preferences, or other profile information to personalize challenges, Ideas, recommendations, content, or other experiences, that information may be used for those purposes.

We may also use aggregated or de-identified information to understand general patterns of use and improve the Service.

12. We Do Not Sell Personal Information

100Daysz does not sell your personal information for monetary consideration.

We do not intend to operate the Service as a data-broker service.

We do not sell age, date of birth, gender, challenge information, or other personal information to data brokers.

If our business model or data practices materially change in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

The current 100Daysz product is intended to be ad-free.

We seek to collect information that is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide the Service;

  • Provide customer support;

  • Resolve disputes;
  • Enforce agreements;

  • Protect the security of the Service.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary, we may delete it, anonymize it, or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal or operational requirements.

28A. India — Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

If you access 100Daysz from India, our processing of your personal data is also intended to align with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), to the extent it applies to the Service. Where the DPDP Act applies, you may have rights to:

  • Obtain a summary of the personal data we process about you and the processing activities;
  • Request correction, completion, or updating of your personal data;
  • Request erasure of your personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal;
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity; and
  • Register a grievance regarding our handling of your personal data.

To exercise these rights or raise a grievance, contact our Privacy & Legal team at admin@100daysz.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

(This section supplements 100Daysz LLC's Privacy Policy to address users located in India and should be reviewed by qualified counsel to confirm full DPDP Act compliance before relying on it.)
