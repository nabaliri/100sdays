Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does 100Daysz work without an internet connection?

Yes. Your challenges, streaks, and notes are stored offline-first on your device, so you can log progress anywhere.

Do I need to create an account?

An account is required to use 100Daysz. It lets us securely manage your subscription and keep your basic profile information. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what we collect.

Is 100Daysz free?

100Daysz is ad-free. Certain premium features are available as an in-app purchase or subscription, managed through the App Store or Google Play.

What devices does it support?

100Daysz is built with Flutter for iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play). Both are launching soon — see the badges above.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account directly in the app, or email us. Full instructions are on our Account & Data Deletion page.
