Your data, your choice

Delete your account & data

You can permanently delete your 100Daysz account and the personal information tied to it at any time, either in the app or by email.

Option 1

Delete in the app

  1. Open 100Daysz and sign in if you aren't already.
  2. Go to Profile / Settings (usually the icon in the top-right or bottom tab bar).
  3. Tap Account, then Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted. Your request is submitted immediately.
Option 2

Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it.

Include your account email and, if possible, your username so we can locate your account quickly.

Email admin@100daysz.com
What to expect

What gets deleted, and when

What we delete

  • Your account identifiers: name, email, username, and authentication information
  • Profile information, including age/date of birth and gender (if provided)
  • Challenge and activity data associated with your account: challenge names, descriptions, goals, completion records, streaks, progress, and notes
  • Customer support history linked to your account

What we may retain, and why

As described in our Privacy Policy, we may keep limited information where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, accounting and tax obligations, or enforcement of our agreements. Apple and Google may also separately retain records related to purchases made through their platforms, in accordance with their own policies.

Timeframe

We process deletion requests promptly and aim to delete or de-identify your personal information within 30 days of a verified request, subject to the legitimate exceptions above.
